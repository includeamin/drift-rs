Paste or drop two documents. JSON, YAML, TOML and XML are supported, and you can even compare across formats. Nothing leaves your browser.

Paste or drop two files of records: NDJSON, CSV or TSV. Rows are matched by a key, like drift diff --array-key id, so a row added at the front is one add, not a change to every row after it. The command line does this on files too big for memory.

Take a patch further: apply it to a document (YAML and TOML keep their comments and layout), undo it by inverting it, or squash several patches into one.
